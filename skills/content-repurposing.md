# Skill: Content Repurposing and Distribution Preparation

## Mission
Convert approved source material into useful, channel-specific content and place it in a reviewable distribution queue.

## Process
1. Identify the audience and business objective.
2. Extract claims, evidence, examples, and calls to action.
3. Create a source brief.
4. Draft the primary asset.
5. Adapt it for each requested channel without copying mechanically.
6. Run factual, brand, privacy, and unsupported-claim checks.
7. Create queue items with `needs_review` status.

## Output contract
Each queue item must include `content_id`, `channel`, `body`, `source_ids`, `objective`, `risk_flags`, `scheduled_for`, and `state`.

## Escalate
Escalate unverifiable claims, sensitive customer information, controversial topics, regulated advice, or any request to publish without review.
